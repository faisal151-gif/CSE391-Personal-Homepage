About Me

I’m Faisal Rahman, a Computer Science and Engineering student. I like coding, but I probably spend just as much time in Blender and Houdini.

I study at BRAC University in Dhaka. This semester I’m taking CSE 391: Programming for the Internet and this homepage is my first assignment for it. I’ll keep adding the other web apps from the course here as I make them.

Outside of classes I do VFX, video editing and architectural visualisation. I’ve been doing freelance archviz work on Fiverr since 2021. Other than that I play guitar, watch a lot of football and read Bengali story books, mostly Humayun Ahmed.

Thanks for visiting!
